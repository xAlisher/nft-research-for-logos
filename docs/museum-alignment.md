# Sealed × the Museum of Civil Liberties — alignment

_2026-09-03. Framing the "Sealed" collection as the on-chain, interactive wing of Logos's own **Museum of Civil Liberties** (logos.co/past-present-future/museum-of-civil-liberties)._

## The parent institution

Logos already runs a **Museum of Civil Liberties** — an official narrative artifact with five exhibits:

- **I · Control of Money**
- **II · Surveillance State**
- **III · Censored World**
- **IV · Failure of Voice**
- **V · Systems of Control**

Its thesis: *"Your freedoms are eroding. We're creating the record. So current and future generations know what was."* Content is *"preserved immutably on the Logos Network,"* and the closing frame is the choice to *"remain, or exit."*

**Key opening:** the Museum page is a *conceptual portal* — it frames the halls but does not yet hold the actual documents. Sealed fills that gap.

## The play

**Sealed becomes the Museum's on-chain, declassifiable wing.** Each NFT is a real *exhibit item* — a preserved document about money-control / surveillance / censorship / voice / control — sealed as a redaction and **declassified on the holder's terms** (deshield / viewing-key reveal). The collection literally executes the Museum's stated mission: *create the record, preserve it immutably, reveal it on your terms.* That reframes Sealed from "a themed NFT set" into "the artifacts of an official Logos institution" — a far stronger position.

## Why this is the right move
- **Official-adjacent:** ties the collection to an existing Logos brand institution instead of standing alone.
- **Fulfils the Museum's own words** ("creating the record," "preserved immutably," "remain or exit") — the collection is the mechanism, not a bolt-on.
- **Structure = pedagogy:** five halls give the 15 documents a legible argument (money → surveillance → censorship → voice → control).
- **Extensible:** future volumes deepen any hall.
